Customize your Ocean Depths storefront without touching a line of code

Ocean Depths runs on Everfront's visual Design panel — the same colors, logo, banner text and homepage layout you'd normally hire a developer to edit are exposed as plain fields, color pickers and on/off switches. This guide walks through every one of them, in order, with exactly what to click.

Installing the theme

This part happens once, before any of the styling below. Four steps: create the store, make sure Ocean Depths is in your template library, assign it to the store, and connect Tebex.

1

Create a store

In the Everfront dashboard, click New store and fill in three things: a Store name (only your team sees this — it just labels the store in the dashboard), a Hostname (this becomes yourname.everfront.store — a custom domain can be attached later), and your Tebex public token.

🔑
Get the Tebex token from Tebex Creator panel → Developers → Headless API. It's safe to paste here — it only grants read access to your public catalogue, never payments or account access.
2

Make sure Ocean Depths is in your template library

Templates live at the workspace level, separately from any one store — open the workspace menu (your workspace name, top-left) → Themes. If Ocean Depths isn't already listed under Your templates, click Import a template and upload it as a ZIP (from BuiltByBit, Polymart, or wherever it was purchased).

3

Assign it to your store

Open the store → Overview tab → find the Template row → click Change. Pick ocean-depths-theme from the list and confirm.

everfront.io/dashboard/stores/your-store?tab=overview
Change this store's template

Your store gets its own copy of whichever template you pick. Its files are yours to edit, editing them changes nothing on your other stores, and the template stays in your library for next time.

ocean-depths-theme — 43 files magma-forge-theme_1 — 31 files

⚠️
This makes a fresh copy of Ocean Depths for this store. If the store already has a template with edits you care about, they're not touched until you actually confirm the swap — but there's no going back to the old copy afterwards, so double-check which store you're in first.
4

Confirm Tebex is connected

Still on the Overview tab, check the Tebex row — it should read Connected with your store's name and currency underneath. This comes from the public token entered in step 1; if it says otherwise, re-check the token in Settings.

5

Set the store logo & tab icon (optional, one-time)

Also on Overview, under Logo and tab icon — this is separate from anything in the Design panel. Logo is a fallback used wherever the template calls for the store logo generically (left blank, it borrows your Tebex account logo). Browser tab icon becomes your favicon — upload anything and Everfront pads it into a 64×64 PNG automatically.

✅
That's installation done. From here, everything else in this guide — colors, hero banner, homepage sections, widgets, footer — happens under the store's Design tab.
Start here

Where everything lives

Every setting in this guide is in the same place. Get here once and you can find any of the sections below in a few clicks.

1

Open your store in Everfront

Go to everfront.io → Dashboard, then click your store's name from the Stores list.

2

Click the "Design" tab

At the top of the store page you'll see four tabs: Overview Design Code Settings. Click Design — that's the no-code panel this whole guide uses. (Ignore Code; that's raw HTML/CSS for developers only.)

3

Pick a sub-tab

Inside Design there are three sub-tabs: Appearance (colors, logo, banner, sections — most of this guide), Widgets (the sidebar), and Pages (the written text on your homepage). Each section below tells you which one to click.

💡
Every field updates a live preview. Nothing you type or click here affects the real store until you press Publish — see step 8. Feel free to experiment.

①Primary Accent is the one field that matters most. It's the color used for every "Buy", "Add to Basket" and "View Store" button, plus all links. Changing just this one swatch is often enough to make the theme feel like a completely different color scheme (blue → green, orange, purple, whatever matches your server).
1

Click a color swatch

Click the small colored square next to any field. A color picker opens — drag to pick a shade, or type a hex code (like #ff6a2b) directly into the text box next to it if you already have one.

2

Watch the preview update

The store preview (Overview tab, or the Preview draft button at the bottom of the page) reflects your new colors immediately — no need to save first.

3

Keep contrast in mind

Text Color should stay light if Background Color is dark (and vice-versa). If in doubt, only change Primary Accent and Gold Accent and leave the rest as-is — that's the safest way to re-brand without anything becoming hard to read.

1

Upload your logo

Click Upload an image under Header Logo and choose the file from your computer — or paste a direct image URL into the field instead. A transparent PNG around 160×40px looks best; the header will scale it to fit automatically.

2

Set your Discord invite

Paste your server's invite link (e.g. discord.gg/yourserver) into Discord URL. This powers the Discord button shown in the top-right of every page.

3

Decide on Sticky Header

Leave this on Yes unless you have a reason not to — it keeps navigation and the basket icon reachable as customers scroll down long pages.

⚠️
Logo — Line 1 / Line 2 do not change what you actually see. The big "OCEAN REALMS" artwork in the banner is a single illustrated image file, not text — those two fields only fill in its invisible screen-reader description. To change the artwork itself, you have to go through the Code tab — see the box below.
1

Set Logo — Line 1 / Line 2 anyway

Even though they don't touch the visible artwork, still fill these in — they become the image's accessibility text (what screen readers announce, and what search engines index), so they should match whatever your logo artwork actually says.

2

Edit the two buttons

Primary Button Text is the main button (usually links to your store — this one is fixed to your packages). Secondary Button Text + Secondary Button URL is a second button you fully control — point it at Discord, a website, a wiki, anything.

3

Hiding the banner entirely

If you'd rather the homepage jump straight into products, set Show Hero to No. This is reversible any time.

The banner's "OCEAN REALMS" wordmark is a pre-made illustration, not live text, so it has to be swapped as a file. Here's exactly where it lives in the theme's source, opened from the store's Code tab (not Design — this one needs the code editor):

{# Logo and CTAs #} <div class="hero-content"> <div class="hero-logo" role="img"> {# Illustrated server-name wordmark. #} <img class="hero-logo-art" src="{{ asset('ocean-realms-wordmark.png') }}"> </div>
①That underlined filename, ocean-realms-wordmark.png, is the actual artwork — a plain image sitting in the theme's assets/ folder.
1

Easiest: replace the file, keep the name

In the Code tab, open the assets folder, click ocean-realms-wordmark.png, delete it, then use Upload files to upload your own artwork saved under that exact same filename. The template's code doesn't change at all — it just picks up the new picture.

2

Or: point the code at a new file

Upload your artwork under any filename (e.g. my-server-logo.png), open webstore/index.html, and change asset('ocean-realms-wordmark.png') to asset('my-server-logo.png') on that line, then click Save file.

3

Sizing

The original is a wide illustrated wordmark. Keep a similar aspect ratio (roughly 5:1, transparent background) so it doesn't stretch or get cropped inside the banner.

04

Homepage sections

Design→Appearance→Sections — every block on your homepage below the hero banner has its own on/off switch. No section can be reordered here, but each can be shown, hidden, or given its own text.

everfront.io/dashboard/your-store/design
Show Featured Packages — spotlighted products on the homepage
Show Store Categories — the category grid
Show Promotional Banner — a sale/announcement strip with its own title, subtitle & background image
Show Recent Payments — live feed of recent purchases
Show Payment Goal — monthly funding progress bar
Show Discord CTA — join-Discord block with its own title & description
Show FAQ — the FAQ accordion
1

Flip any switch to Yes / No

Each row is independent — hide the FAQ if you don't have one yet, keep Featured Packages and Categories on, and so on. The homepage reorders itself automatically; there's nothing else to configure for most of these.

2

Fill in the sections that ask for text

Promotional Banner and Discord CTA unlock extra fields (title, subtitle/description, and for the banner, an optional background image) once you switch them on — write your own sale message or Discord pitch there.

1

Reorder by dragging

Click and hold the grip handle (⠿) on the left of any widget row, then drag it up or down. The sidebar on the live store updates to match this order top-to-bottom.

2

Hide a widget without deleting it

Use the switch on the right of each row to turn a widget off temporarily — the trash icon next to it removes it completely, which you can always undo with Add widget.

3

Write your own "Welcome" text

Click the Welcome row to expand it — this is a free text box for server rules, announcements or links, shown as plain text or simple HTML at the top of the sidebar.

4

Add more widgets

Click + Add widget to bring in ones you haven't used yet (Community goal, Featured package, Gift card balance...) — some only appear on the live site once there's real data behind them (e.g. Top donator needs at least one payment).

07

Page text

Design→Pages — the written paragraphs on your homepage (and any custom page you add), edited like a simple document.

1

Open the Home page

Under Pages, click Home page — you'll see its address (yourstore.everfront.store/) and a Content editor below it with headings like "Welcome", "How it works", "Get your name right".

2

Edit the text directly

Click into any paragraph and type — this is a normal text editor, no code involved. Use it to explain what purchases support, how long delivery takes, or anything specific to your server.

3

Add a new page

Click New page to create additional pages (rules, a FAQ, a refund policy) — each gets its own title, its own address, and the same simple text editor.

08

Preview & publish

Everything above is saved as a draft automatically as you type. Your live store is untouched until you explicitly publish.

1

Check your draft

Click Preview draft (bottom of the Design panel) to open your changes exactly as a customer would see them — without affecting the live site.

2

Publish when you're happy

Once the preview looks right, use the publish action next to the "Live on v8 · published…" status line to push your draft live. The timestamp updates to confirm it went through.

3

Changed your mind?

Every field can simply be typed over again — there's no limit to how many times you republish, and nothing here is destructive to your products, payments or Tebex setup.

✅
Almost everything lives in these few screens. Colors, header logo, hero text and buttons, homepage sections, sidebar widgets, footer and page text are all no-code. The one exception is the hero banner's illustrated wordmark artwork itself, which needs a quick trip to the Code tab — see that box above.
FAQ

Troubleshooting

?

I changed a color and part of the site still looks wrong.

Make sure you're looking at Preview draft, not the live store — unpublished changes never show on the public URL. If the preview itself looks off, check that Text Color still contrasts against your new Background/Surface colors.

?

My logo looks blurry or squished.

Re-upload it as a PNG close to 160×40px with a transparent background — very large or oddly-shaped images get scaled down unevenly.

?

A widget isn't showing on the live store.

A few widgets (Top donator, Community goal, Recent payments) only render once there's real Tebex data behind them — they're not broken, they're just empty until a payment or goal exists.

?

I need something this panel doesn't cover.

Structural changes — new page layouts, custom components, anything beyond colors/text/toggles — go through the Code tab instead, which is raw HTML/CSS and is best left to a developer.
